## WF02 / approved scope continuation
Discrete viewpoint assets are declared in scene data, not embedded in the navigation controller. Last-place return records the last non-Atelier location and viewpoint; a new tab still opens in the Atelier. Helm's existing event visualizations are made directly discoverable. No final room geometry or stylistic realization has been selected.

## WF03 interaction and Helm composition
- Atelier quick actions move to the central lower region. A 380 ms hover dwell opens their interface; click remains immediate and touch remains click-only. Delay is provisional pending ergonomic evaluation.
- Helm presents its own sky-focused observation space, not a view of Hermitage architecture. The shared sky's actual-time phase should eventually be supplied by the canonical world/lighting service.
- Helm scheduling retains the shared event repository and expands historical Quiet Hours views incrementally, rather than embedding a second data store.

## WF05: Integration boundary
Preserve the existing Living Pool unchanged as a bundled same-origin application. Embed it reversibly rather than rewriting its calendar and audio for this release. Preserve the historical Quiet Hours planner separately until its algorithm can be tested against Supeesu2 shared records.


## WF06 — Restoration over approximation
The original Quiet Hours Helm visual and interactive implementation is the authority for the Helm experience. Restore its original star map and editor instead of replacing them with simplified imitations. The original planner remains separate at the data-contract boundary until its disruption overrides can be represented faithfully in shared data. Embedded Pool navigation must not initiate standalone automatic audio. Hover-dismiss is enabled only for panels opened by hover; deliberate interaction pins them.

## WF07 — Long Voyage authority
*Observatory — The Long Voyage* is the most recent confirmed Helm artistic direction. Quiet Hours is the functional source for scheduling, not the competing visual design. Preserve the original visual composition and connect instruments to real schedule views. Cohesion through relationships, not uniformity; emotional truth can supersede literal physical realism. The 36-hour chapter cycle remains experimental.

# Lighting and materials — Environment 06

The real local clock applies a final comfort presentation over ecological world lighting. The World Clock, ecological chronology, fish scheduling, audio positions, and real countdowns are not modified.

Canonical values are in `shared/lighting/comfort-policy.json`, relative scene illumination only, not monitor luminance. The principal darkening interval is 21:30–23:00. By 20:30–21:00 the scene should feel nocturnal; deepest hours should favor warm burgundy/red. The red night is retained through 05:29 and the release starts at 05:30.

The sky, ground, water and glints respond separately. The current transformations are provisional: material-specific art-directed shaders and animated plant shadows are deferred. Exceptional violet/gold and silver nights remain future environment treatments, subject to the real-time comfort policy.

Actual emitted light depends on the display. This policy is not a medical intervention or a calibrated brightness controller.
